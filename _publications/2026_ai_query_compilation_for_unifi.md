Proposes compiling hybrid AI queries—integrating relational SQL constructs and LLM inference layers into a unified tensor compute graph—to eliminate CPU-accelerator data movement bottlenecks and accelerate execution on TPUs (up to 5.3× latency and 9.8× throughput speedups).
