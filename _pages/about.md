Welcome! I am a Senior Research Scientist and Technical Lead in the **Systems Research Group (SRG)** at **Google Cloud**. My research explores the intersection of **data management systems**, **machine learning infrastructure**, and **specialized hardware accelerators**.

My work focuses on two core questions:
* **Hardware-Accelerated Analytics:** How can we co-design analytical query execution directly with modern AI hardware accelerators and ML compilers to bridge relational data processing and tensor runtimes?
* **AI for Data Systems & Agents:** How can we build compound AI architectures, natural language data interfaces (NL2SQL), and lightweight surrogate operators that are orders of magnitude faster, cheaper, and more robust?

## Research Interests
* **Hardware-Accelerated Analytics & Query Compilation:** Bridging database query processing with AI hardware accelerators and ML compilers (JAX/XLA) to compile SQL and AI into unified execution graphs.
* **Natural Language to SQL (NL2SQL) & Data Agents:** Large-scale schema linking, agentic reasoning, and long-context architectures for natural language interfaces to complex enterprise data.
* **Proxy AI Operators & Semantic Optimization:** Approximating expensive row-wise LLM semantic operations (`AI.IF`, `AI.RANK`) using lightweight surrogate models over embeddings to slash cost and latency by >100×.
* **Agent Serving & Multi-Objective Scheduling:** Stage isolation and DBMS-style multi-objective query optimizers for compound AI workflows to balance latency, cost, and answer quality.
